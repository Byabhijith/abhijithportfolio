About — 01

I have a philosophical brain.

I'm a motion designer specializing in cinematic short form video, motion graphics and visual storytelling. I work with After Effects, Illustrator, Photoshop, Premiere Pro and Blender to create videos that communicate ideas and emotions with intention and depth.

I don't just execute briefs — I think about the feeling behind the message and build visuals that carry that feeling to the viewer. Every project I take on gets original thinking, not templates.

How I think — 02

Logic beneath feeling.

My work isn't decoration. It's a thought process made visible. Every frame answers a question I asked before the animation started.

Original thought, not templates
  1. 01

    Feel first

    Before a single keyframe, I sit with the message. What should the viewer carry away? The feeling is the brief.

  2. 02

    Write the philosophy

    I script in language, not storyboards. Scripts carry rhythm, silence and intent — the visuals inherit that weight.

  3. 03

    Design the atmosphere

    Palette, texture, type and sound are chosen to serve the emotion. Every frame is a small room the viewer steps inside.

  4. 04

    Animate the meaning

    Timing is the language of motion. I animate so the idea lands on a breath — not on a beat.
